# Prompts para gerar as imagens do robô Zé da Bomba

Use os prompts abaixo em uma IA de imagem (Midjourney, DALL·E, Gemini, Leonardo, Firefly etc.). Em inglês costumam funcionar melhor; há uma versão em português no final. Salve os resultados com os nomes `ze_esboco_v1.png`, `ze_esboco_v2.png` e `ze_esboco_v3.png` na raiz do repositório para substituírem os esboços no README.

O Zé da Bomba é um **humanoide parcial** (no estilo do Pepper/Nao) que atende em um **eletroposto** (recarga de carros elétricos): cabeça com dois olhos de LED, tronco e dois braços com garras mecânicas, sobre uma base com rodas. **Sem pernas, sem pele, sem boca e sem rosto realista.**

---

## V1 — Esboço em papel com formas primitivas (protótipo no papel)

```
Hand-drawn pencil sketch on white grid paper of a partially humanoid service robot that works as an attendant at an electric vehicle charging station, built only from primitive shapes. A wide low cylinder base with three small wheels and a thin light ring around its edge (no legs). A slightly tapered cylinder torso about 0.8 m tall with a small logo of a pump with a lightning bolt, the words "ZÉ DA BOMBA", a red emergency button and three small rectangular tool holders on the front (squeegee, tire inflator, fluids kit). A short neck and a rounded oval head with a dark visor showing two round LED eyes (no mouth, no nose, no skin). Two articulated arms drawn as cylinders with spheres for shoulder, elbow and wrist joints, ending in mechanical C-shaped grippers: the right arm holds a thick EV charging connector (CCS2) with a heavy cable, the left arm is raised holding a small tablet with an amber light on top, as a "stop here" gesture. Total height about 1.5 m. Handwritten annotations with arrows in Portuguese: "olhos de LED", "LiDAR", "tablet + luz âmbar", "garra segurando o conector de recarga", "porta-ferramentas", "parada de emergência", "anel de luz", "base com rodas", "1,50 m". Engineering notebook style, black pencil with a few blue and yellow highlighter accents, front view, clean and legible.
```

## V2 — Cena de uso (esboço colorido)

```
Colored marker concept sketch, industrial design style, of a friendly partially humanoid robot attendant named "Zé da Bomba" at a covered electric vehicle charging hub next to a Brazilian gas station at dusk. The robot is about 1.5 m tall, white and blue, with a rounded head and a dark visor showing two glowing cyan LED eyes (no mouth, no face details), a torso with a pump-and-lightning-bolt logo, two mechanical arms with grippers, and an omnidirectional wheeled base with a blue light ring partially filled like a battery gauge. It stands diagonally next to the open driver window of a compact electric hatchback, holding a 10-inch tablet with large buttons "Recarregar", "Para-brisa", "Fluidos" and "Calibrar" and a battery percentage, about 50 cm from the window, while its head turns toward the driver, a man in a wheelchair-adapted car. Its other arm has just plugged a thick charging cable from a tall DC fast charger into the car's charging port. A dashed yellow rectangle is projected on the ground around the working area. In the background, a human station operator in uniform watches from the next charger holding a radio. Three-quarter view, marker rendering with soft shadows, warm and trustworthy but clearly mechanical. Not an android, no human skin.
```

## V3 — Render final do produto

```
Photorealistic 3D product render of a partially humanoid service robot for electric vehicle charging stations named "Zé da Bomba", in the style of Pepper or NAO but industrial. Height 1.5 meters. Glossy white shell with deep blue and yellow accents, a small logo of a pump with a lightning bolt and the words "ZÉ DA BOMBA" on the torso. Rounded head with a black glass visor showing two round cyan LED eyes, no mouth, no nose, no skin; a small LiDAR sensor and a thermal camera on top. Tapered torso with a big red emergency stop button and a front tool holder with a squeegee, a tire inflator nozzle and a small fluids kit. Two articulated robotic arms in matte grey with visible shoulder, elbow and wrist joints, ending in insulated mechanical C-shaped grippers; one gripper holds a thick CCS2 EV charging connector with a heavy cable, the other holds a slim 10-inch tablet with a card reader. Omnidirectional wheeled base with a thin blue light ring, no legs. Industrial look with small high-voltage safety labels. Studio lighting, neutral light grey background, three-quarter front view, high detail, sharp focus, 4k.
```
